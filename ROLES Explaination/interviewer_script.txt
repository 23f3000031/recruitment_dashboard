=== RECRUITPRO INTERVIEWER PANEL SCRIPT ===

OVERVIEW:
RecruitPro Interviewer Panel - Interview management interface for hiring panel members.

ACCESS:
- URL: http://127.0.0.1:5000/login
- Credentials: interviewer@recruitpro.com / interviewer123
- Role: Interviewer (limited access focused on interview activities)

MAIN FEATURES:

1. DASHBOARD OVERVIEW
   - View interview-specific KPIs
   - Metrics: Today's interviews, upcoming interviews, completed interviews
   - Interview count statistics

2. INTERVIEW MANAGEMENT
   - View all scheduled interviews
   - Filter interviews by date, status, candidate
   - View interview details (date, time, round type, meet link)
   - Access interview feedback forms
   - Submit feedback ratings (1-5 stars)
   - Provide recommendations (Strongly Hire / Hire / Maybe / No Hire)
   - Add interview notes and comments

3. INTERVIEWER-SPECIFIC FEATURES:
   - Submit feedback for conducted interviews
   - View interview details and candidate information
   - Access meet links for scheduled interviews
   - View candidate information and resume basics
   - Submit rating for communication, technical skill, problem-solving, cultural fit
   - Submit overall recommendation

4. INTERVIEWER LIMITATIONS (vs Recruiter/Admin):
   - Cannot add or manage candidates
   - Cannot schedule new interviews (already scheduled only)
   - Cannot access candidate full profiles (limited info)
   - Cannot access VCT verification
   - Cannot manage assessments
   - Cannot generate documents
   - Cannot access job postings
   - Cannot access system settings
   - Cannot manage user accounts

5. QUICK ACTIONS:
   - View today's interviews
   - Submit interview feedback
   - Access scheduled interviews
   - View interview details
   - Submit ratings and recommendations

NAVIGATION:
- Sidebar shows: Dashboard, Coding Sandbox, Interview Schedule, Notifications
- Settings link NOT visible
- Candidate management NOT available
- VCT Verification NOT available
- Queue management NOT available
- Assessment management NOT available
- Document management NOT available
- Reports limited view only

FLASH MESSAGES:
- Feedback submission confirmations
- Interview status updates
- Reminder notifications
- Error messages for invalid actions

USE CASES:
- Interviewer conducting scheduled interviews
- Providing structured feedback on candidate performance
- Rating candidate skills and providing hiring recommendations
- Accessing interview details and meet links
- Viewing interview history and status

ACCESSIBLE PAGES:
- Dashboard (interviewer view)
- Interviews list
- Interview detail page (with feedback form)
- Coding Sandbox
- Notifications

RESTRICTED PAGES:
- Candidates, Candidate Detail
- VCT Verification
- Queues, Assessment management
- Documents generation
- Job postings
- User management/Settings
- Advanced analytics

FLow:
1. Interviewer logs in
2. Views today's/interviewer's interviews
3. Clicks on interview detail
4. Submits feedback (ratings + recommendation)
5. Recommendation may auto-move candidate to next stage
6. Feedback stored and visible to recruitment team

SCREEN FLOW:
- Login → Interview Dashboard → Select Interview → Submit Feedback → Confirmation

BENEFITS:
- Structured feedback collection
- Consistent rating criteria
- Hiring decision support
- Interview process documentation
- Team collaboration on candidate evaluation

RECOMMENDED WORKFLOW:
1. Interviewer logs in via http://127.0.0.1:5000/login
2. Clicks on "Interview Schedule" in sidebar
3. Selects interview to review
4. Submits feedback form with 1-5 ratings
5. Provides hiring recommendation
6. Clicks submit → Feedback saved → Candidate stage may update
7. Returns to interview list
